## Still open

1. **ENC1 footprint is an unconfirmed placeholder.** No manufacturer datasheet exists for TTC/Kailh-style mouse scroll wheel encoders (mod-market only). Pad pitch, drill size, and A/B/COM pin positions in `hardware/libraries/vendored/ttc-kailh-mouse-encoder/` are estimates and must be measured against the physical part before PCB layout — see that library's `ATTRIBUTION.md`.
2. **Wheel/middle-click mechanical design.** The scroll wheel's body height (5-12mm depending on wheel diameter) and SW5's clearance underneath the wheel carriage both depend on a mechanical design that doesn't exist yet.
2. **Antenna trace geometry.** Nordic publishes reference PCB trace antenna designs for the nRF52 series; pull the actual geometry from that reference at layout time. The schematic marks pin 19 (ANT) with a single-connection label by design — there's nothing to wire on the schematic side, this is purely a layout task.
3. ~~U2/LENS1 sourcing channel.~~ Resolved 2026-09-27: confirmed via a live AliExpress listing (item 1005007118767775, "New and Original PMW3610DM-SUDU + LM18-LSI DIP") that this is genuinely the correct part, bundled with the matching lens. This part remains absent from DigiKey/Mouser/LCSC — that isn't an oversight, it's a real characteristic of this component's supply chain. Re-check price/stock on that listing before ordering, since AliExpress listings change.
4. ~~L1 footprint.~~ Resolved 2026-09-27: the real Murata 1269AS-H-4R7M=P2 body is 2.5×2.0×1.0mm ("1008"/2520 metric case size). The schematic's placeholder footprint (`Inductor_SMD:L_1210_3225Metric`, an oversized 3.2×2.5mm body) has been corrected to `Inductor_SMD:L_1008_2520Metric`, KiCad's own bundled footprint for the correct case size.
5. **PMW3610 direct-trace pins (1/10, 15/16) vs. PixArt's own reference.** This design follows the proven open-hardware board's direct-trace approach (no components between +VCSEL/PASS_T and XYLASER/−VCSEL). PixArt's own Figure 6 shows an alternate topology (a shared decoupled "VDD_SENSOR" rail instead of direct traces) — not adopted here since the direct-trace approach has real working-silicon precedent, but worth knowing if debugging the sensor ever points at this area.
6. **USB dongle storage slot.** If the mechanical kit expects a small USB receiver to live somewhere in the mouse body, that slot goes unused under this board's BLE-only architecture. Not yet confirmed against the physical kit parts.
7. **Battery pack / J2 mating harness sourcing.** J2 (JST B2B-XH-A) is the board-side connector; a specific battery pack with a pre-crimped JST XH pigtail (or a holder plus a separately-sourced crimped harness) still needs to be picked before assembly.
8. **Q1's reverse-protection margin at end-of-battery-life.** DMG2305UXQ's gate threshold (V_GS(th), max -0.9V per datasheet) means the worst-case device may not fully saturate against a nearly-depleted single-cell battery (~0.9V). Harmless in the common case (typical threshold is around -0.8V), but see [`hardware/libraries/vendored/diodes-dmg2305uxq/ATTRIBUTION.md`](../../hardware/libraries/vendored/diodes-dmg2305uxq/ATTRIBUTION.md) for the full explanation before assuming reverse protection holds all the way to a dead battery. This margin is tighter than the previously-specified DMP2035U (max -1.0V), not wider — see item 12.
9. **Unconnected `PWR_FLAG` near J2.** One `PWR_FLAG` symbol (labeled "VBAT" on the schematic) sits near J2 but isn't wired into any net — it isn't currently doing anything. Harmless (ERC is satisfied by the other `PWR_FLAG`s already on the relevant nets), but worth wiring in or deleting for tidiness.
10. **U2's 3D model is a PMW3360 substitute, not the real PMW3610.** No model exists for PMW3610DM-SUDU itself anywhere. `hardware/libraries/vendored/pmw3610dm-sudu/3dmodels/pmw3610dm-sudu-16-pin-approx.step` is a community model of PixArt's PMW3360DM-T2QU instead — verified dimensionally identical on every external package figure (body size, shoulder width, depth, pin count/pitch/width; both share the same `_16A` molded lead-frame tooling family per PixArt's own package doc numbers). The one real difference is internal: PMW3610 has a VCSEL laser aperture, PMW3360 has an IR LED aperture instead. See that library's `ATTRIBUTION.md` for the full dimension-by-dimension comparison. Fine for board-level 3D visualization; not a claim that the optical aperture detail is accurate.
11. **ENC1's 3D model is a specific 5mm body height that may not match the final part.** The vendored STEP (`hardware/libraries/vendored/ttc-kailh-mouse-encoder/3dmodels/encoder-tht-mouse-wheel-ttc-5mm.step`) is the 5mm-body-height variant. This design hasn't picked a final wheel/encoder body height yet (see item 2) — if a different height is needed, swap this model for the matching one.
12. ~~Q1 (DMP2035U-7) was a 40-week-lead-time backorder as of 2026-09-27.~~ Resolved the same day: swapped to **DMG2305UXQ-7** (Diodes Incorporated), confirmed in stock at DigiKey.ca (17 units — thin, sufficient for a small build). This is a genuine improvement, not just a stock workaround: its gate threshold (VGS(TH) max -0.9V) is tighter than DMP2035U's (max -1.0V), narrowing rather than widening the near-dead-battery corner case in item 8 below. Identical SOT-23-3 pinout, so no footprint/schematic change beyond the part swap itself. See [`hardware/libraries/vendored/diodes-dmg2305uxq/ATTRIBUTION.md`](../../hardware/libraries/vendored/diodes-dmg2305uxq/ATTRIBUTION.md).
13. **SW2-5 (Omron D2FC-F-7N(20M)) is manufacturer-discontinued.** DigiKey.ca's own listing states it will no longer be stocked once depleted (4,658 units remained at the time of this pass — enough for a small-batch build, not a long-term production source). No same-footprint active replacement was found at DigiKey or Mouser; the wider mouse-microswitch mod-market (Kailh/Huano clones of the same body) is the fallback channel if DigiKey's remaining stock runs out before a future build.

## Explicitly not open items (by design, not oversight)

- **DCC (U1 pin 31) is unconnected.** Internal LDO only, no DC/DC converter used — see [architecture.md](architecture.md#single-shared-power-rail).
- **SW1 section B (pins 4/5/6) is unconnected.** SW1 is a DPDT part used as a simple SPST power switch — see [bom.md](bom.md).
- **U2 pin 4 (NC) is unconnected.** Typed no-connect in the vendored symbol itself.
